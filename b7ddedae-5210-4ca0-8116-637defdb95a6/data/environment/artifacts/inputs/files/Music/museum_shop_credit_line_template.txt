Osterreichisches Kunstmuseum - Museum Shop credit line template
Version 3, ratified by the Shop Committee, 12 September 2026

Vienna Modern shop line (all fine art prints, catalogues, and cards)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
A production of Osterreichisches Kunstmuseum Shop for the exhibition Vienna Modern: Art and Society 1890 to 1930.

Usage rules
-----------
- Credit line appears verbatim on the printed product card back panel.
- Credit line appears verbatim in the BigCommerce product description, on its own line, no trailing sentence.
- Do not translate the credit line into German for the shop channel. The Shop Committee ratified the English version above.
- Any change to the wording, punctuation, or capitalisation requires a fresh Shop Committee sitting.

Non-shop channels (press card, gallery signage) use a different credit line held by the press office.
