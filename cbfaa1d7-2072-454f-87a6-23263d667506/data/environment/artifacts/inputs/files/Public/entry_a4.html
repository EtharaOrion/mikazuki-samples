About

I am a board-certified music therapist working at the intersection of clinical neurorehabilitation and private practice. Music, for me, is the bridge between injury and recovery, isolation and connection.

I trained as a concert pianist at Ashvale Conservatory, earning a Bachelor of Music in 2012 and a Master of Music in 2014, before clinical work redirected my path. I completed my MA in Music Therapy at Emberglen University in 2018 and hold MT-BC board certification through CBMT.

Music bridges language and silence, injury and recovery, more than concert prestige ever did.

Approach

Neurologic Music Therapy treats music as a precise clinical tool, not decoration. Rhythm reorganizes movement; melody re-opens language.

Gait Recovery

Rhythmic auditory stimulation entrains a steadier, more symmetrical walking pattern for patients relearning to move after stroke.

Aphasia

Melodic intonation therapy uses the intact musical systems of the brain to rebuild functional speech after language loss.

Motor Return

Therapeutic instrument playing turns reaching, grasping, and pressing into musically motivated, repeatable movement.
