# Heartbeat: Nicole King

## Recurring Events

### Daily
- **Every day, 5:45 AM (yoga days) or 6:30 AM**: Wake. Phone glance, weather, the day's schedule.
- **Every day, 1 to 2 hours**: Piano practice on the Steinway. Morning or evening, never skipped.
- **Every day, 8:00 AM and 7:00 PM**: Email inbox sweep, structured like practice blocks.

### Weekly
- **Monday, Wednesday, Friday, 5:30 AM**: Yoga prep. Mat and water bottle for the 6:30 AM class at Modo Yoga.
- **Monday through Thursday, 8:30 AM**: Leave for Kestrel Healthcare. F train, 30 minutes door to door, 9 AM to 5 PM clinical day.
- **Tuesday and Thursday, 6:00 PM**: Private practice prep. Studio ready, last session notes reviewed, two clients 6:30 to 8:30 PM.
- **Thursday, 5:45 PM**: Quiet space for telehealth therapy with Dr. Walsh at 6:00 PM via Zoom.
- **Friday, 10:00 AM**: Anne check. Visit every other Friday; call on the off weeks.
- **Saturday, 8:45 AM**: Piano practice 9:00 to 11:00 AM. Sacred, non-negotiable, no interruptions.
- **Saturday, 12:30 PM**: Studio reset for private practice clients 1:00 to 4:00 PM.
- **Sunday, mid-afternoon**: Cooking and meal prep for the week. Instacart order lands earlier in the day.

### Monthly
- **1st of each month, 10:00 AM**: Private practice billing review, insurance submissions, CMTE credit log update, Plaid summary read.
- **1st Sunday of each month, 2:00 PM**: Trio rehearsal in the living room with Lena and David. Tea and scores ready by 1:30 PM.

### Quarterly
- **Once per quarter**: OT appointment with Dr. Hoffman at Montrose Medical. Afternoon off from Kestrel Healthcare.
- **Once per quarter**: Steinway tuning with Daniel from Ashvale Piano Services, $250.
- **Once per quarter**: Private practice insurance billing batch submission.

- **April 15**: Federal tax filing deadline, Schedule C for the private practice.

- **July 4**: Matt's family barbecue in New Jersey.
- **July**: Nicole and Matt anniversary, plans vary year to year.

- **October**: Kestrel Healthcare annual performance review with Dr. Lieberman.
- **Late November**: Thanksgiving at Matt's parents' in New Jersey.
