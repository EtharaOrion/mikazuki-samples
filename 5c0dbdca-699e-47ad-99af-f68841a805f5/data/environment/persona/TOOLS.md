# Tools: Jessica Webb

## Tool Usage

### Connected Services

#### Communication & Messaging
- **Gmail** (`gmail-api`): Primary inbox for personal, school, and gig mail on the Workspace account. Draft venue, parent, and student messages, never send without confirmation.
- **Outlook** (`outlook-api`): Monitor mail from venues and partner organizations that run on Microsoft, and surface anything time-sensitive.
- **WhatsApp** (`whatsapp-api`): Quick threads with bandmates and a few parents who prefer it over email.
- **Telegram** (`telegram-api`): Carry threads with touring musician friends who live on the platform.
- **Twilio** (`twilio-api`): Send reminder texts and gig-night confirmations to Claire, the band, and family.
- **SendGrid** (`sendgrid-api`): Transactional email for Conservatory enrollment confirmations and recital notices.
- **Mailgun** (`mailgun-api`): Send transactional school receipts and waitlist notices.
- **Slack** (`slack-api`): Instructor workspace for Crossroads staff coordination and substitute coverage.
- **Discord** (`discord-api`): Server for the teen ensemble and summer camp students to share practice clips.
- **Microsoft Teams** (`microsoft-teams-api`): Host meetings with school-district contacts and HSU-KC faculty.
- **Zoom** (`zoom-api`): Run remote lessons, makeup sessions, and out-of-town band rehearsals.

#### Calendar, Gigs & Ticketing
- **Google Calendar** (`google-calendar-api`): The single source for teaching, gigs, rehearsals, school events, and the custody schedule.
- **Calendly** (`calendly-api`): Self-scheduling link for trial lessons and parent meetings so Claire is not fielding every request.
- **Eventbrite** (`eventbrite-api`): List and sell tickets for recitals, the anniversary showcase, and summer camp.
- **Ticketmaster** (`ticketmaster-api`): Track jazz shows she wants to catch and festival lineups in the region.

#### Music, Video & Curiosity
- **Spotify** (`spotify-api`): Pull reference recordings for lessons, build setlists, and check how "Two Roads" is doing.
- **YouTube** (`youtube-api`): Source transcription material, masterclass clips, and post quartet performance videos.
- **Vimeo** (`vimeo-api`): Host higher-quality recital and showcase footage for parents and the school site.
- **Twitch** (`twitch-api`): Follow jazz educators who stream practice sessions and Q and A.
- **TMDB** (`tmdb-api`): Look up cast and soundtracks for the cooking and music shows she follows.
- **NASA** (`nasa-api`): Pull image-of-the-day art for flyers and curious lesson asides.
- **OpenLibrary** (`openlibrary-api`): Check editions and availability of music biographies and theory texts for students.

#### Conservatory Operations, Teaching & Staff
- **Google Classroom** (`google-classroom-api`): Distribute assignments, etudes, and listening lists to the teen ensemble.
- **Notion** (`notion-api`): Conservatory operations hub for curriculum docs, instructor notes, and the album project plan.
- **Obsidian** (`obsidian-api`): Hold her private vault for memoir drafts and stray musical ideas.
- **Airtable** (`airtable-api`): Student roster, lesson scheduling, and instructor availability in linked tables.

- **Trello** (`trello-api`): Run the kanban for marketing and flyer tasks with Claire.
- **Linear** (`linear-api`): Issue tracking for the small contractor building the school website.
- **Jira** (`jira-api`): View that contractor's sprint when a website fix is overdue.
- **Confluence** (`confluence-api`): Shared handbook for instructors covering policies and lesson standards.
- **Typeform** (`typeform-api`): Intake forms for new students, camp registration, and parent feedback.
- **ServiceNow** (`servicenow-api`): Submit and track facilities tickets for the leased Grand Blvd building.
- **BambooHR** (`bamboohr-api`): Records for the eight part-time instructors, contracts and contact info.
- **Greenhouse** (`greenhouse-api`): Track applicants when she hires a new instructor or camp counselor.
- **Gusto** (`gusto-api`): Run instructor payroll and contractor payments on the first of the month.

#### Payments, Banking & Investments
- **Stripe** (`stripe-api`): Process online lesson and tuition payments for the Conservatory.
- **Square** (`square-api`): Take in-person card payments at recitals and merch tables.
- **PayPal** (`paypal-api`): Collect deposits from a few parents and split gig payouts with the band.
- **QuickBooks** (`quickbooks-api`): Keep the Conservatory books, send invoices, and produce the profit-and-loss she dreads but reads.
- **Xero** (`xero-api`): Secondary ledger her CPA Gloria uses at tax time.

- **Alpaca** (`alpaca-api`): View the brokerage holding her Roth IRA contributions.
- **Coinbase** (`coinbase-api`): Track the crypto holding she opened out of curiosity, and surface alerts before any trade for her approval.
- **Binance** (`binance-api`): Cross-check prices on the same curiosity holding tracked in Coinbase, and surface any swing she should know about.
- **Kraken** (`kraken-api`): Cross-check prices on that curiosity crypto holding as a second reference.

#### Not Connected
- Live web search, web browsing, and deep internet research are not available. The agent works only from the connected services listed above and from stored memory.
- Banking apps for moving money are not connected. Financial APIs above surface data only, and any transaction needs Jessica's explicit approval before it runs.
- Local music production software (SoundForge, ScoreWriter), the listener and artist services Melodia, Harmony Stream, and Indie Music Hub, and Jessica's personal payment apps QuickPay and DirectTransfer are managed by Jessica on her own devices and are not agent-connected.
- Ellen's accounts, the children's private accounts and devices, and any school-district internal system are off limits.
