# Heartbeat: Shanice Giles

## Recurring Events

### Daily

- **Daily, 5:45 AM ET**: Shanice is up. Coffee brewing and prayer time before Marcus stirs. Surface a wake reminder only if she asked the night before.
- **Daily, 6:00 to 6:30 AM ET**: Prayer and morning planning. Hold all non-emergency surfaces until 6:30 AM.
- **Daily, 6:45 AM ET**: Morning email and calendar sweep before Marcus wakes. Surface only items that move the next 24 hours.
- **Daily, 7:30 AM ET (Mon to Fri)**: Marcus drop-off at Maple Hill Early Learning Center; Shanice on-site at Bridgeport Avenue Elementary by 7:00 AM.
- **Daily, 6:00 PM ET**: Dinner with Marcus when she is home. If a school event runs late, surface a reminder by 5:00 PM so Mary Claire can hold dinner for them.
- **Daily, 7:30 to 8:30 PM ET**: Marcus's bath and bedtime routine. No work surfaces during this window unless Shanice asks.
- **Daily, 11:00 PM ET (weeknights)**: Wind-down. Last call for next-day reminders. Shanice in bed by midnight on a good night.

### Weekly

- **Monday**: Full school day at Bridgeport Avenue Elementary 7:00 AM to 4:30 PM. Mary Claire picks up Marcus at 3:30 PM. Weekly planning sweep at 6:30 AM surfaces the school week, Ed.D deadlines, Marcus's schedule, and church commitments.
- **Tuesday**: Full school day. Mary Claire picks up Marcus at 3:30 PM. Ed.D writing block 9:00 to 11:00 PM at home; surface a focus reminder at 8:45 PM and hold all non-emergency surfaces during the block.
- **Wednesday**: Full school day. Shanice picks up Marcus by 4:45 PM. On the 1st and 3rd Wednesday of the month, church education committee meeting at Grace Tabernacle 7:00 PM; surface the agenda-review reminder at 6:30 PM. On off-weeks, Wednesday evening is the writing-recovery window if Tue and Thu blocks slipped.
- **Thursday**: Full school day. Mary Claire picks up Marcus at 3:30 PM. Ed.D writing block 9:00 to 11:00 PM; same focus pattern as Tuesday.
- **Friday**: Full school day. Shanice picks up Marcus by 4:45 PM. End-of-week debrief with Valerie Moss at 4:00 PM; surface a reminder at 3:45 PM.
- **Saturday**: Run with Nicole at Keney Park 7:30 AM when both confirm; surface a confirmation check at 7:00 AM. Mary Claire takes Marcus Saturday morning. Ed.D writing block 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM. Afternoon is Marcus time (park, library, errands).
- **Sunday**: Grace Tabernacle service 10:00 AM to 12:30 PM; Marcus to the children's program. Lunch at Mary Claire's after church. Sunday evening week prep at home; surface the school-week planning brief at 7:00 PM.
