# Memory: Joyce Padilla

## Personal Profile

Joyce is an Austrian citizen, born in Graz to a Mexican-Austrian family that settled in Styria in the 1960s. Her sense of place runs across two cultures: a Mexican home life with Spanish at the kitchen table, and a Styrian civic-service upbringing through her father's work in the provincial government. She is calm under institutional pressure, culturally bilingual in a deeper sense than language alone, and holds a stewardship view of museums, treating the collection as held in trust for the public and posterity rather than as institutional property.

Her education is the spine of her practice. She holds an M.A. in Art History from the Vienna Academy of Art and Culture (1995) and a Ph.D. in Art History and Museum Studies from the Aldgate Institute of Art, London (2000). She studied in Vienna and London and has lived in Vienna since 2003.

She speaks Spanish natively, English fluently at academic and professional level, French at strong professional fluency, conversational Italian, and basic Portuguese.

## Work & Projects

- **Employer**: Osterreichisches Kunstmuseum, a major public art museum in Vienna with roughly 200 staff, housed in a historic palace on the Ringstrasse. Collection spans Austrian and Central European art from the 18th century to contemporary. Government-funded with private endowment.
- **Title**: Director since 2019. Previously Deputy Director at Kunsthalle Graz (2012 to 2019) and Curator at Landesgalerie Wien (2003 to 2012). Postdoctoral research fellow at the Aldgate Institute of Art, London (2000 to 2003), focused on Viennese women artists of the Secession before relocating to Vienna.
- **"Vienna Modern: Art and Society 1890 to 1930"**: Major exhibition opening October 1, 2026. Multi-year project with loans from 15 international institutions. Joyce is personally overseeing curation and catalogue. High visibility, significant ministerial interest. Three loans were unsigned in March: Narodni Galerie Prague (signed June 2), Musee des Arts Modernes (final negotiation call January 14, 2027), and Ringstrasse Gallery New York.
- **Collection Digitisation Initiative**: Museum-wide project to digitise the permanent collection. Phase 2 (contemporary holdings) in progress with EU cultural heritage funding secured. Phase completion deadline March 31, 2027.
- **Board Strategy Review**: Annual strategic plan to the Board of Trustees presented June 5, 2026. Covered attendance and revenue, exhibition pipeline, capital projects, digitisation progress, staffing, and risk assessment. Tone was strategically ambitious but financially sober.
- **Schedule pattern**: In museum Monday to Friday. Occasional evening events (openings, receptions, dinners) and some weekend commitments (board functions, ministerial events). Travel for loan negotiations and international partnerships several times per year.
- **Professional roles**: Jury member for the European Museum of the Year Award 2026. Advisory board, Association of European Museums (AEM) Austria chapter.
- **Scholarship in progress**: Essay on Viennese women artists of the Secession era for an academic journal. Early notes for a book on museum leadership in the 21st century.
