Profile

A champion for the students who need one most.

Building principal with fourteen years in Hartford public education, now leading a Title I elementary school of more than four hundred students in the West End. My work centers on three convictions: that structured literacy is an equity issue, that families are partners rather than audiences, and that school culture is built through restorative - not punitive - practice.

Since taking the principalship in 2022, I have moved the school from reactive crisis management toward a stable, data-informed improvement cycle: rising ELA proficiency, a 34% drop in suspensions, and parent-council attendance up from 14 to 22 per session. I am completing an Ed.D in Educational Leadership, studying how principals in under-resourced schools sustain instructional focus under accountability pressure.
