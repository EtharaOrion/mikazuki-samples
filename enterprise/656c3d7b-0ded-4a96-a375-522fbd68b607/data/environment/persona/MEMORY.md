# Memory: Dorothy Langston

## Personal Profile
- **Ethnicity**: Anglo-American
- **Occupation**: Associate Professor of Computational Biology at Haverstone University; classical ballet dancer and instructor
- **Education**: B.Sc. in Biochemistry from Thornfield University (1999); Ph.D. in Computational Biology from Ashvale Technology Institute (2005)
- Raised in Boston; family roots in New England. Quietly proud of her academic trajectory and her long ballet career.
- Identifies as a scientist first, dancer second, though both shape how she moves through the day.

## Work & Projects
- Associate Professor of Computational Biology at Haverstone University since 2017; on faculty for nine years. Joined directly from a combined postdoctoral and assistant-professor track (2005 to 2017) following her Ph.D.
- Runs the Langston Lab: two postdocs, three PhD students, and a lab manager. Focus on structural genomics and protein interaction networks.
- **Tenure review** scheduled for the 2026 to 2027 academic year. The defining professional stress of her life.
- **Whitfield Foundation for Science** grant: principal investigator, $420,000 over 3 years, ending March 2027.
- **National Genomics Consortium** grant: principal investigator, $285,000 over 2 years, ending December 2026.
- **Voss collaboration**: co-authored paper on allosteric regulation in protein interaction networks, in revision at The Ashford Journal of Molecular Biology (three revision rounds so far).
- Teaches an advanced ballet class at Thornfield Ballet Academy on Saturdays as a parallel professional identity.
