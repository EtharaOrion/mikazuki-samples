# Heartbeat: Joyce Padilla

## Recurring Events

### Daily
- **Every morning**: Up at 6:15 AM, a 45-minute walk through Josefstadt and the Ringstrasse, then strong morning coffee. Two to three coffees across the day. Surface the day's agenda, high-stakes meetings, and overnight institutional activity at the start of the session.
- **Every evening**: Bed by 11:00 PM with reading before sleep. Wine with dinner most evenings, one or two glasses.

### Weekly
- **Monday**: Up at 6:15 AM with the walk, in the museum by 8:30 AM. Executive team meetings in the morning, Pilates at 12:30 PM at Studio Koerperwerk in the 7th district, afternoon meetings and correspondence, home by 6:30 PM. Surface the week-planning prompt at 8:00 AM to review museum calendar, board items, and personal schedule for the week.
- **Tuesday**: Museum day for strategic planning and curatorial team meetings. Biweekly therapy with Dr. Lukas Mayer at 6:00 PM, home by 7:30 PM. Surface the therapy reminder at 5:30 PM and clear the calendar around it.
- **Wednesday**: Museum day for external meetings: ministerial, partner institutions, donors, often as lunch meetings. Mid-week call with Gloria at 11:00 AM to ask about the house and her reading.
- **Thursday**: Museum day for collection and conservation focus. Pilates at 12:30 PM, board preparation work in the afternoon, occasional cultural events in the evening. Surface the correspondence review at 5:00 PM to clear pending emails and draft responses for external partners.
- **Friday**: Lighter museum day for administrative wrap-up, correspondence, and reading. Weekly wrap-up at 3:00 PM to review open items, update exhibition tracking, and prepare for next week. Often leaves by 4:30 PM.
- **Saturday**: Slightly later start, then swim at Josefstadt Schwimmbad at 8:00 AM (surface the prep reminder at 7:30 AM with swim bag check). Cafe Palais for coffee and papers, errands at the Naschmarkt, cooking, and an evening with Matthias for concert, dinner, or a quiet night in.
- **Sunday**: Slow morning with a walk. Occasional museum visits or gallery openings. Weekly call with Gloria at 11:00 AM. Family dinner at home, weekly planning, and Felix's cello practice in the late afternoon.

### Monthly
- **1st of each month**: Financial review. Verify the EUR 2,000 savings transfer, Lena's EUR 800 support payment, and the household accounts all reconciled.
- **Monthly**: Visit Gloria in Graz for a long weekend, weather and rail schedule permitting.

### Quarterly
- **Quarterly**: Board of Trustees meeting with the 12-member board. Track agenda items and follow-ups as institutional commitments through the cycle.

### Seasonal / Variable
- **Winter (November to March)**: Vitamin D supplement daily, evening reading windows extend, and heating maintenance on the apartment scheduled by Hausverwaltung.
- **Summer**: Museum closed one week for maintenance, Joyce and Matthias take their main holiday in the Salzkammergut.
