Our story

Named for Eleanor

Eleanor's Tavern opened on the corner of 30th Avenue in 2015, named for the grandmother whose pot roast still sets the standard in the kitchen. Carlton runs the room, his brother Daniel runs the line, and a core team has stayed together for years because loyalty is the way the place works.

The menu leans into classic American tavern cooking, hearty starters, clay pot dishes, grilled fish, and pies made in house. The patio opens with the warm season, April through October, and the dining room hums every night from five.
