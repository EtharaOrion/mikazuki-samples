The Program

About the Class

What

A two-hour advanced ballet class held every Saturday morning, emphasizing technical refinement, musicality, and classical choreographic style. The class moves through a full barre sequence before transitioning to center work, petite allegro, grand allegro, and combinations from the standard classical repertoire. Students are expected to arrive warmed up and to maintain consistent attendance.

Who

This class is designed for students with a minimum of six years of formal training who are working at or above an intermediate-advanced level. Prior pointe work experience is required for students who wish to participate in the pointe portion of the class. Adult students with equivalent training backgrounds are welcome. Placement for new students is by brief interview or observation during the first session.

Why

Ballet at the advanced level is not only about technique - it is about understanding the tradition well enough to inhabit it fully. Students in this class will develop a stronger command of classical style, greater physical efficiency, the ability to respond to live accompaniment, and a working knowledge of the choreographic forms that define the canon. The class is small by design: no more than twelve students, so each receives meaningful individual attention.

Program Structure

Curriculum

  • Extended warm-up sequences including plies in all five positions with full port de bras
  • Battement tendu and degage combinations emphasizing placement and weight transfer
  • Rond de jambe work focused on hip rotation mechanics and spatial awareness
  • Fondu, frappe, and adagio sequences drawn from Vaganova and Cecchetti traditions
  • Port de bras and epaulement combinations drawn from the classical repertoire
  • Adagio center with arabesque, attitude, and pirouette preparation
  • Pirouette technique: en dehors, en dedans, preparation and finish
  • Petite allegro and jumping combinations with emphasis on landing mechanics and musicality
  • Pointe barre warm-up with emphasis on intrinsic foot strength and articulation
  • Bourree and pas de bourree sequences across the floor
  • Variation study: excerpts from the standard classical canon such as the Sylphides repertoire and the classical pas de deux tradition
  • Individual coaching on variation corrections and stylistic detail
  • Each term includes one short choreography project in which students learn and refine a set piece
  • Focus on spatial awareness, use of the stage, and the relationship between movement and musical phrasing
  • Informal showing at the end of each term for students who wish to perform for family and academy community
  • Discussion of score analysis: understanding how choreographic structure relates to musical form
Your Instructor

Dorothy Langston

DL
Dorothy Langston
Advanced Class Instructor

Dorothy Langston has been training and performing in classical ballet for thirty years. She began formal study as a child and continued through her university years, training under the direct mentorship of Patricia Irving, the founder of the Thornfield Ballet Academy. Over those decades she has performed in classical repertoire, participated in faculty demonstrations, and maintained a committed practice that has not diminished alongside a demanding professional life in science.

By day, Dorothy is an Associate Professor of Computational Biology at Haverstone University, where she directs a research laboratory focused on protein interaction networks and structural genomics. She holds a Ph.D. from the Ashvale Technology Institute. She brings to her teaching the same qualities she values in research: precision, patience, attention to structure, and the conviction that understanding why something works is inseparable from training until it does.

She believes that an advanced student is not someone who has finished the fundamentals - it is someone who has begun to understand them deeply enough to make them disappear into something that looks like dancing. Saturday mornings at Thornfield are dedicated to that conversation.

Please bring both pointe shoes and flat ballet slippers to each session.
