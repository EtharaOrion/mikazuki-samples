# Memory: Amanda Tran

## Personal Profile
- Amanda Tran is a Vietnamese-American gospel choir director whose joy fills a room before she speaks and whose work flows from her faith.
- Cultural identity: Vietnamese-American, raised in the church, fluent in the traditions of both her heritage and her gospel calling; she anchors the family's Lunar New Year, Mid-Autumn Festival, and Easter celebrations.
- Education: Master of Music Education from Atlanta State University, and a Bachelor of Arts in Music from Heritage Women's College, the same college her daughter Grace now attends.
- Temperament: a commanding presence who can silence a 45-voice choir with a raised hand, nurturing to every member, disciplined in rehearsal but warm enough that it feels like fellowship.
- Philosophy: service is her calling and the church is her extended family; she leads with encouragement and treats music as ministry.
