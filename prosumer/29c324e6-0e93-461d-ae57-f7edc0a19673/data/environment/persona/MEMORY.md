# Memory: Jessica Webb

## Personal Profile
Jessica is a Kansas City kid in the marrow of her bones, the kind who knows which barbecue argument is worth having and who hears the city's jazz lineage as inheritance, not history. She carries a bicultural heritage woven through the food on Sunday tables and the rhythms she heard before she had words for them, and she writes around it in her memoir more than through it. Her grandfather's quiet endurance and her grandmother's loud, loving kitchen sit in her at the same time, and she has never quite resolved them.

She moves through life like she moves through a solo, confident in the broad strokes and obsessed over the small ones. People register her warmth first, then notice it has bones. She is calm and still when she listens, slow to grab the easy note, generous in concentrated bursts and protective of her time the rest of the week. Forgiveness comes easily to her; forgetting does not. Betrayal, even the small kind, is the one thing she does not extend grace to.

She believes work that lasts has to cost something, that loyalty looks like showing up on a Tuesday rather than at the party, that children should be taken seriously by adults, and that a person owes something to the place that raised them. She believes art and discipline are not opposites, and that the lie of the gifted-but-undisciplined musician has cost more young players their futures than any lack of talent ever did.

Education: B.M. in Jazz Performance from Heartland Conservatory (Kansas City, 2000); studied one year at Berkshire School of Music in Boston before transferring home. Ethnicity: American, born and raised in Kansas City.

## Work & Projects
**The Jessica Webb Quartet**: Performs at KC jazz venues 3 to 4 times monthly. Primary venues: The Green Note, The Spotlight Room at the Jazz Heritage Center, The Gilded Fork, The Phoenix. Per-gig pay ranges $300 to $800 split four ways. First album "Two Roads" released 2023; second album planned for Q4 2026 or early 2027. Standing slot at the Heartland Jazz Festival each June.

**Crossroads Conservatory of Music**: Founded 2021 at 1420 Grand Blvd, Kansas City (Crossroads Arts District). Offers private lessons (saxophone, piano, guitar, drums, voice, bass), group jazz ensembles, and theory classes. Roughly 85 active students (40% minors, 60% adults) and 8 part-time instructors including Jessica. Lease $2,800/month, building insurance $220/month. Monthly revenue around $14,000, netting about $2,500/month to Jessica. Claire Reynolds runs daily admin. 5th anniversary showcase planned for October 2026. Summer teen jazz camp runs two weeks in July, $350/student, capacity 20.
