# Memory: Nicole King

## Personal Profile
- **Full name**: Nicole King, "Nikki" to family and close friends, born and raised in Park Slope, Brooklyn.
- **Identity arc**: Perfectionist in slow recovery from a twenties spent chasing technical perfection on the piano at every cost.
- **Pivot moment**: Tendinitis at 28 and burnout at 29 redirected her from concert performance to clinical music therapy work.
- **Emotional style**: Processes feelings through music before words; deeply empathetic, absorbs client pain and decompresses alone at the keyboard.
- **Social pattern**: Warm and articulate in a room, then withdraws for two days with the Steinway to recharge fully.
- **Cultural identity**: Layered Brooklyn upbringing across traditional family values, secular progressivism, and a quiet personal contemplative streak.
- **Faith practice**: Attends Park Slope Community Church occasionally, drawn more to the ritual and community than to doctrine itself.
- **Education (high school)**: Midwood High School, Brooklyn, graduated 2008, before two performance degrees at Ashvale Conservatory followed in sequence.
- **Education (conservatory)**: Earned BM Piano Performance at Ashvale Conservatory in 2012, then MM Piano Performance there in 2014.
- **Clinical credentials**: MA Music Therapy from Emberglen University in 2018, plus MT-BC Board Certification through CBMT that same year.
- **Philosophy**: Believes music bridges language and silence, injury and recovery, isolation and connection, more than concert prestige ever did.

## Work & Projects
- **Primary role**: Music Therapist (MT-BC) at Kestrel Healthcare's Manhattan neurorehabilitation unit, full-time Monday through Thursday, 9 AM to 5 PM.
- **Caseload**: 18 to 22 patients at a time across stroke recovery, traumatic brain injury, and neurodegenerative disease conditions.
- **Techniques**: Core Neurologic Music Therapy: rhythmic auditory stimulation for gait, melodic intonation therapy, therapeutic instrument playing for motor recovery.
- **Documentation**: Clinical notes live in Epic EHR, which is off-network here; treat that side as wholly separate.
- **Supervisor**: Dr. Sarah Lieberman, Director of Rehab Services at Kestrel; monthly supervision, warm and demanding mentor relationship.
- **Reputation**: Kestrel Healthcare neurologists request Nicole by name, a fact she notices quietly but does not say aloud.
- **Admin load**: Insurance prior authorizations, justification letters, and denial appeals make up her least favorite part of the role.
- **CMTE cycle**: 42 of 100 credits completed; cycle closes December 31, 2027; Ashvale and AMTA are likely sources.
- **Private practice**: Park Slope home studio, second bedroom converted, upright Yamaha, rhythm instruments, and therapy-friendly seating throughout the space.
- **Client mix**: Three children on the autism spectrum and two to three adults working anxiety or depression musically each week.
- **Schedule**: Sessions run Tuesday and Thursday 6:30 to 8:30 PM and Saturday 1:00 to 4:00 PM weekly.
- **Rate**: $150 per 50-minute session; private practice income supplements the Kestrel Healthcare salary materially each month.
- **Active arc**: Dr. Lieberman pushing Nicole to write a case series from her aphasia melodic-intonation therapy work.
