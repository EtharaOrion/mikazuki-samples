# Memory: Carlton McGee

## Personal Profile

Carlton is a first-generation Queens New Yorker, son of a Boston-formed family, and the grandson of Eleanor McGee, whose name lives over the door of his restaurant. He moves through the world with a dining-room presence that fills a room before the door closes behind him: loud, fast, warm without performing it, and capable of holding three conversations at once without anyone feeling shorted. Underneath is a worrier who carries the math on rent and labor in his shoulders and his jaw, and who has been on the restaurant high wire long enough to know exactly how far the fall would be.

He is Episcopal by culture more than creed; he attends St. Andrew's on Easter and Christmas Eve, says the prayers from memory, and treats the liturgy as a shape that holds him rather than a theology he is still unpacking. His New England formation shows in the accent that comes back when his mother is on the phone, the Boston loyalty (Celtics, Sox), the Sunday roast inheritance, and the very specific way he holds a wooden spoon at the stove. His humor is fast and sometimes too pointed; the version of him at forty-four is gentler than the version at thirty-four, and he can tell the difference even when others cannot.

Education: B.S. Hospitality Management from Empire State Hospitality Institute (2003), plus a culinary training program at New England Culinary Institute (Vermont summer program, age 20, 2002). His father Christopher died in 2019 of a heart attack at 70; Carlton carries that grief as a thing he holds rather than processes, and the restaurant is part of the carrying.

## Work & Projects

Carlton worked across NYC restaurants from BS completion in 2003 through 2014; moved to Astoria at 26 in 2007, bought the corner that became Eleanor's Tavern, and opened in 2015 at age 33. The restaurant is named for his late paternal grandmother. He handles business operations, front-of-house, vendor relationships, and hiring. Staff total around 18 across Carlton (owner and front), Daniel (head chef), Sophia Kastner (manager), 2 line cooks, 1 prep cook, 4 servers, 2 bussers, 1 dishwasher, 1 bartender, and 2 part-time hosts. The cuisine is traditional and modern American tavern: grilled meats and fish, hearty starters, clay-pot dishes, house-made pies, an American wine and craft beer list. Seating 65 indoor and 24 patio (seasonal, April through October).

Service: lunch Tue to Fri and dinner Tue to Sun, with Monday closed. Restaurant email carlton@eleanorstavern.com is managed separately from the assistant's connected workspace.

Current projects: fall and winter menu rollout, renegotiating fish pricing with Kevin Walsh for Q4 2026, the American craft wine tasting series (next event scheduled for late 2026), and planning the patio expansion build-out for the 2027 season. Revenue runs ~$1,350,000 a year gross. Expenses ~$1,150,000 (labor $480K, food and bev $405K, rent $144K, utilities $42K, insurance $24K, misc $55K). Net income roughly $200,000 a year before personal taxes.
